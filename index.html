MARKET RULES

One hour. Your call.

The 60 minute playfield includes 15, 30, and 60 minute prediction intervals. Up wins when the closing benchmark is at least the opening benchmark; Down wins when it is lower.

Contract prices follow uncalibrated model estimates from trailing 24-hour volatility and time remaining. These are estimates, not guarantees. Contracts return $1 per share if correct and $0 otherwise. Winning returns round up to the next cent. There are no fees or real-money orders.

Your full budget is committed. Predictions close 30 seconds before each interval ends. Historical views and synthetic previews cannot place orders. Your account saves orders and retries settlements when benchmark data is missing.
