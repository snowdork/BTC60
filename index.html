MARKET RULES

A clear outcome, every interval.

Chance estimates: 50/50 at the starting benchmark. A simple volatility-and-time calculation shifts the estimate as spot moves above or below that center. These are uncalibrated estimates, separate from demo contract quotes. Upcoming intervals show a neutral baseline; completed intervals show their result.

Up / Yes wins when the final benchmark is greater than or equal to the starting benchmark. Down / No wins when it is lower. There is no separate tie outcome.

For Kalshi’s 15-minute crypto markets, the benchmarks use the average of the CF Benchmarks real-time index during the minute before the market opens and the minute before it closes. Spot price is context; it does not determine settlement by itself.

This independent demo proposes the same comparison for 30- and 60-minute intervals. These are sample markets, not verified Kalshi listings. Upcoming intervals have no benchmark or executable quote until they open.

Demo contracts cost the displayed cents per contract and return $1 if correct, $0 otherwise. Whole contracts only; unspent budget stays in the demo balance. Demo fees are $0. Selected-coin spot and hourly history come from Coinbase when connected. Benchmarks shown are illustrative minute-candle proxies, not the CF Benchmarks settlement index. Contract quotes and all orders remain simulated. Signed-in predictions and demo settlements are stored by the local server; settlements use Coinbase minute-close proxies, not official exchange results.
