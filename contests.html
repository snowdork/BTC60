Only announced, reserved pools award prizes. Records start in review; an operator checks eligibility and arranges delivery. Game credits cannot be redeemed.

    Contest rules and prize details

    Rule version: utc-net-credits-v1. Contest mode has a fixed 10,000-credit spending allowance per GMT day. Winnings affect rank, but do not replenish that allowance. Practice funds and predictions never affect contest rankings. No purchase, ad view, or ad click is required.

    Spend at most 1,000 credits per instrument and exact interval, counting both directions and repeated entries. Existing prediction cutoffs apply. Score is settled returns minus settled costs; pending results are excluded until settled. The highest final score wins, even when all scores are negative. At least one settled entry is required.

    Intervals belong to the GMT hour and day immediately before their closing boundary. A 23:45–00:00 prediction counts for the day just ended. All first-place ties share rank. Reserved pools are divided between tied winners; indivisible remaining cents go in ascending account-ID order. If a pool has fewer cents than tied winners, some winners receive no gift-card award. With no participants no prize is awarded.

    Daily contests run from 00:00 GMT to the next 00:00 GMT; hourly contests close at each HH:00 GMT. GMT is fixed at UTC+00:00 and does not shift for daylight saving. Missing price history delays final results and prize allocation until all entries settle; there is no automatic timeout or invented result. Open and awaiting-settlement standings are not final winners. Historical practice activity is not imported.

    Gift cards are the only prize option. Prize pools are shown in USD. Prize descriptions must state eligibility, provider, region, and redemption restrictions before entries open. If no pool is displayed, the contest awards recognition only. Approved or fulfilled records reflect operator actions, not an automatic cash-out facility. Rejected prizes are not automatically redistributed.
