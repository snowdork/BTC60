    Hourly contest rules

    Rule version: hourly-net-credits-v2. Each GMT hour has a fresh 1,000-credit spending allowance across all instruments, intervals, and directions. Winnings count toward your tally and cannot be spent again. Unused credits do not carry forward. Practice keeps its separate balance. Daily contests are disabled.

    Your tally is settled returns minus settled costs. Pending entries remain provisional. The highest final net tally wins, even if all tallies are negative. Ties share first place. Hours with no entries have no winner. Intervals ending at HH:00 belong to the hour just ended.

    At each hour boundary, entries close, all predictions settle, and final results and winners are saved before the next allowance unlocks. Missing price history delays both the winner and the credit reset; results are never invented. Hours stay aligned to GMT (UTC+00:00), without daylight-saving shifts.

    Only announced gift-card pools award prizes, subject to operator eligibility review and delivery. Tied winners split the pool; leftover cents go in account-ID order. Game credits are not redeemable for cash. Historical records retain their original rule version and are not recalculated under the new allowance.
