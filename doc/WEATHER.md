Weather is a dormant category marked **Coming soon**. Users cannot enter a weather terminal or place weather orders. `weather.seed.json` defines New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, Miami, Seattle, Denver, Boston and San Francisco. These are a default selection of major US cities, not a popularity ranking.

`weather.py` builds 20 disabled instruments: temperature at 2 m in °C and sustained wind speed at 10 m in m/s for each city. Intervals are exclusively 60 minutes, aligned to UTC hours; city time zones are for future display. Up means the closing observation is at least the opening observation; Down means it is lower. Negative temperatures and zero wind are valid.

The future provider adapter must pin a station for each city and provide actual observations with `cityId`, `metric`, `stationId`, `observedAt` (UTC epoch seconds), `value`, `unit`, and `kind='observation'`. Benchmarks use the latest observation at or before each boundary, no older than 10 minutes. Forecasts, mismatched stations/units and future observations are excluded. Missing or conflicting latest observations defer settlement. Persist both benchmark observations, station, provider and settlement version with each order to ensure reproducible results.

Before enabling trading, connect and validate an observation provider, assign station IDs, persist observation history and order sources, add weather-specific pricing and UI, and wire the benchmark helpers into order validation and settlement. The existing financial log-return model requires positive prices and must not be reused for temperature or calm wind. This preparation deliberately does not generate fake quotes or enable orders.
